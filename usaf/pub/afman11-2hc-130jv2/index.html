AFMAN11-2HC-130JV2 — Hc -130j--aircrew Evaluation Criteria

AFMAN11-2HC-130JV2 governs HC-130J aircrew evaluation criteria, including evaluation requirements and grading areas.

It covers instrument, qualification, mission, formal course, instructor, weapons instructor, emergency procedures, special qualification, and instructor certified event evaluations. It requires compliance with the publication and specifies evaluation methods, graded areas, and minimum emergency procedure evaluations.

Applies to: It applies to all civilian employees and uniformed members of the Regular Air Force, Air Force Reserve (AFR) and Air National Guard (ANG) performing aircrew duties in HC-130J aircraft.

Questions and answers

When can an instructor evaluation area be omitted?

Grade if observed. Not required if evaluated as part of the EPE.

What happens when a critical evaluation item is failed?

Areas listed as “Critical” represent items that are critical to safety of flight and failing these items results in a qualification level of 3. (paragraph 2)

Can flight personnel perform non-flying duties?

FPs are authorized to perform flying and non-flying duties. (paragraph 3)

How often are loadmaster areas evaluated inflight?

Are evaluated inflight at least every other evaluation. (paragraph 1)

When is an event required for evaluation?

Only required if qualified or certified in this event. (paragraph 1)
