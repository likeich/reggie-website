AR 600-8-105 covers travel, manning, operational support, reassignment, promotions, recruiting, training, and mobilization orders. It requires prescribed forms and formats and establishes requirements for preparing, correcting, filing, and reviewing orders.

Questions and answers

What are orders used to authorize?

Orders are published to (1) Order individuals onto active duty or change the status of MILPER on active duty. (paragraph 2-1)

How must orders be prepared?

Orders are prepared using prescribed forms and formats. (paragraph 2-2)

What do travel orders direct?

Travel orders direct the movement of unit or personnel from one location to another. (paragraph 7)

When is DD Form 1610 used for TDY orders?

DD Form 1610 will be used to request and publish TDY orders unless Format 400 is more applicable. (paragraph 12-1)

What form authorizes emergency leave travel?

DA Form 31 will be used to authorize emergency leave travel to a Soldier or Soldier traveling with Family members. (paragraph 10-5)
