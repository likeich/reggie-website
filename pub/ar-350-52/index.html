AR 350-52 — Army Training Support System

AR 350-52 governs Army Training Support System programs on Army controlled installations and the allocation of TSS resources.

  • Publication number: AR 350-52
  • Title: ARMY TRAINING SUPPORT SYSTEM
  • Date: 01/17/2014
  • Proponent: G-3/5/7
  • Status: ACTIVE

View AR 350-52 on armypubs.army.mil

It covers Army training, force generation, training support programs, training facilities, live, virtual, and constructive integration, and program management. It establishes requirements for delivering training products, services, facilities, and realistic training conditions.

Applies to: This regulation defines responsibilities and prescribes policies of Army Training Support System (TSS) programs on Army controlled installations to ensure that TSS resources are allocated properly to support the Armys training strategies.

Questions and answers

What programs make up the Army Training Support System?

The TSS consists of five primary programs that complement each other and together generate the Armys TSS capability through the delivery of products, services, and facilities. (paragraph 1-11)

What training environments does the Mission Command Training Support Program provide?

The MCTSP provides virtual, constructive, and gaming training environments in support of combined arms training that replicates Army operations across the spectrum of conflict. (paragraph 1-11)

What does the Sustainable Range Program improve?

The SRP is the Armys overall approach for improving the design, management, usage, and long-term sustainability of ranges. (paragraph 1-11)

What does the Training Information Infrastructure Program provide?

The Training Information Infrastructure Program provides the hardware, software, and communications systems, conforming to both Joint and Army architectures and standards that enable the development, storage, retrieval, delivery, and management of training information for use by individuals, units, and institutions worldwide. (paragraph 3-6)

What must the Live, Virtual, Constructive Integrated Training Environment ensure?

The LVC ITE ensures LVC TADSS and embedded training systems are mutually compatible so that they can communicate with each other. (paragraph 3-20)
