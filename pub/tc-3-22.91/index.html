TC 3-22.91 — Mortar Fire Direction Procedures (this Item Is Published W/ Basic Incl C1)

Questions and answers

What does fire direction include?

Fire direction is the tactical and technical employment of firepower, the exercise of tactical command of one or more units in the selection of targets, the massing or distribution of fire, and the allocation of ammunition for each mission. (paragraph 2-1)

What data does a firing table provide?

An FT provides the data required to bring effective fire on a target under any conditions. (paragraph 3-28)

What is required for FDC certification?

FDC certification is required for all units with mortars; it tests the proficiency of Soldiers to perform their duties as FDC computers and section leaders. (paragraph 3-33)

How is data recorded when using the MFCS?

Data is recorded throughout the process to maintain a record and to preserve data if the MFCS fails. (paragraph 9-52)

What is the standard smoke mission coverage and duration?

The standard smoke mission covers 500 meters for 10 minutes, but this width and time can be adjusted depending on the mission requirements. (paragraph 10-107)
