AR 210-130 — Laundry And Dry Cleaning Operations

AR 210-130 governs Army installation laundry and dry cleaning operations, including privatization, services, facilities, funding, and reporting.

It covers GOGO, GOCO, COCO, and mobile field unit operations, service categories and priorities, equipment, facilities, payroll deduction, individual piece rate, and cash per bundle service. It requires cost recovery for reimbursable services, procedures for complaints and claims, inventory controls, transportation restrictions, and installation reporting.

Questions and answers

When was CONUS installation laundry and dry cleaning support privatized?

Effective FY 2002, CONUS installation L&DC support was privatized. (paragraph 1-5)

What types of operations provide laundry and dry cleaning service?

The types of operations that will provide L&DC service are (1) GOGO. (2) GOCO. (3) Contractorowned and contractoroperated (COCO). (4) Mobile field units. (paragraph 2-1)

What must laundry and dry cleaning procedures ensure?

All procedures must ensure that accounting for pieces is completely accurate. (paragraph 2-1)

How must reimbursable laundry and dry cleaning services be funded?

All costs for this type of service must be fully recovered. (paragraph 2-3)

Can new laundry and dry cleaning facilities be built or expanded?

No new facilities will be built or expanded. (paragraph 3-5)
