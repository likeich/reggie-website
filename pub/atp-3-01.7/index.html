ATP 3-01.7 — Air Defense Artillery Brigade Techniques

ATP 3-01.7 governs ADA brigade techniques and organization supporting ADA battalions and batterys.

It covers ADA brigade operations, operational concepts, employment guidelines, counterair operations, mission command, joint operations area defense, and sustainment. The ADA brigade integrates ADA assets, allocates available assets, deploys early warning systems, coordinates airspace control activities, and recommends AMD tactics and techniques.

Applies to: ATP 3-01.7 is for commanders, staffs and Soldiers assigned to the ADA brigade to support the ADA battalions.

Topics covered

  • ADA brigade organization
  • Air and missile defense operations
  • Counterair operations
  • Airspace mission command
  • Joint operations area defense

  • ADA brigade sustainment

Questions and answers

How many producers and query nodes can TIBS support?

The TIBS can support up to 10 producers, 50 query nodes, and an unlimited number of receive-only users. (paragraph 1-8)

What security level does TIBS operate at?

The TIBS operates at the SECRET collateral level. (paragraph 1-8)

What must the ADA brigade operational concept be like?

The operational concept must be uniformly known and understood throughout the armed services on how air defense operates and its contributions as a joint force partner. (paragraph 1-8)

What criteria are used to develop air defense priorities?

ADA doctrine outlines three criteria for developing AD priorities. (paragraph 2-28)

What must ADA command posts be able to do?

Command posts must be able to execute current and future operations and pass orders to subordinate ADA units. (paragraph 3-4)
