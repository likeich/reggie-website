Questions and answers

What counts as a change in the status of a military unit?

Any action that changes the organizational structure, designation, assignment, or location of a military unit is a change in the status of that unit. (paragraph 2-2)

Why are unit status changes recorded?

Unit status changes are made a matter of record for legal and historical purposes. (paragraph 2-2)

What unit actions does CMH record?

CMH records constitutions, activations, inactivations, designations, redesignations, consolidations, reorganizations, assignments, permanent changes of station, and similar actions concerning the unit and its organic elements. (paragraph 2-2)

What must orders announcing significant unit status changes include?

All orders announcing significant changes must include CMH (ATMHHPO) in distribution. (paragraph 2-2)

How is the effective date of a unit status change determined?

The effective date is the date specified in the orders or the date of the orders if no effective date is specified. (paragraph 2-2)
