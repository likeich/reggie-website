TC 3-21.220 covers basic and advanced airborne training, parachutes, equipment, aircraft, drop zones, water operations, and emergency procedures. It establishes training standards, personnel qualifications, equipment requirements, aircraft and drop zone procedures, and malfunction reporting requirements.

Applies to: TC 3-21.220 applies to the Active Army, Army National Guard/Army National Guard of the United States, United States Army Reserve, Department of the Navy, Department of the Air Force, and the United States Marine Corps, unless otherwise stated.

Questions and answers

What is the purpose of airborne training in TC 3-21.220?

The purpose of airborne training is to qualify personnel in the use of the parachute as a means of combat deployment. (paragraph 1-1)

What wind limit applies to T-11 and MC-6 parachutes?

The T-11 and MC-6 are limited to operation in winds of 13 knots at the surface. (paragraph 2-1)

How many personnel are required to don parachutes?

Two personnel are required when donning parachutes. (paragraph 2-12)

Which jumpers must use T-11 reserve inserts?

The use of T-11 reserve inserts (see figure 2-1 on page 4) with the T-11 reserve parachute are mandatory for all primary jumpmasters (PJMs), assistant jumpmasters (AJMs), and the No. 1 jumper assisting in pushing a door bundle. (paragraph 2-5)

What are the maximum allowable surface winds for static line personnel airdrops?

Maximum allowable surface wind for static line parachute personnel airdrops is 13 knots (17 knots for a WDZ). (paragraph 21-20)
