Questions and answers

How must laundry and dry cleaning workload be accounted for?

Separate accounting, abstracting, and summarizing the workload will be done for each type and category of work processed (i.e., laundry or dry cleaning, reimbursable or nonreimbursable). (paragraph 2-1)

How often must laundry and dry cleaning data reports be submitted?

The data collected per paragraph 14b (1) will be consolidated into a report covering the quarters ending 31 December, 31 March, 30 June, and 30 September.

When must the quarterly laundry and dry cleaning report be sent?

The report will be sent no later than the 15th calendar day after the close of the quarter to the major command (MACOM) staff activity having jurisdiction over the operation of the Army installation L&DC facility.

What checks may be accepted for piece-rate and cash-per-bundle laundry services?

Only personal checks in amounts not to exceed laundry or dry cleaning charges and instruments made payable to the Treasurer of the United States will be accepted. (paragraph 2-3)

How often must collected laundry and dry cleaning money be deposited?

All monies, except those authorized for retention in charge funds, will be deposited daily or at least weekly. (paragraph 2-4)
