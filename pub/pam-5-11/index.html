Questions and answers

What are the goals of the Army M&S Enterprise approach?

The goals of the enterprise approach are identifying and mitigating enterprise-level capability gaps, minimizing redundancy, and promoting reuse. (paragraph 2-2)

How often must organizations review their Army M&S Repository data?

Each organization with M&S equities will conduct a bi-annual Army M&S repository review to ensure relevancy and accuracy of data (see para 3 2). (paragraph 2-2)

What does the M&S 3-star GOSC do?

The M&S 3-star GOSC validates and prioritizes resource requirements and mitigates capability gaps through policy changes and funding decisions. (paragraph 2-3)

Who is eligible for the Army M&S Awards program?

Participation in the Army M&S Awards program is limited to U.S. military and DoD civilians. (paragraph 8-2)

Does the AMSO develop Army M&S standards?

The AMSO does not develop M&S standards for the Army. (paragraph 7-1)
