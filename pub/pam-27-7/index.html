PAM 27-7 — Guide For Summary Court-martial Trial Procedure

PAM 27-7 governs procedures for fair and impartial Summary Courts-Martial trials and related trial duties.

  • Publication number: PAM 27-7
  • Title: GUIDE FOR SUMMARY COURT-MARTIAL TRIAL PROCEDURE
  • Date: 04/02/2014
  • Proponent: TJAG
  • Status: ACTIVE

View PAM 27-7 on armypubs.army.mil

It covers key personnel, trial procedure, evidence, motions, witnesses, pleas, findings, sentencing, and post-trial actions. It requires the SCM officer to conduct proceedings fairly, prepare and authenticate the record, and forward the original record to the convening authority.

Questions and answers

What is the SCM officer responsible for during a trial?

The SCM officer is the presiding officer at all sessions of the trial and is responsible for ensuring that the proceedings are conducted in a fair, orderly, and dignified manner in accordance with law. (paragraph 1-3)

What legal questions can the legal advisor answer?

The legal advisor will answer any questions of law or procedure which arise. (paragraph 1-3)

Does an accused have the right to counsel at a Summary Court-Martial?

The accused at a SCM does not have the right to counsel. (paragraph 1-3)

What evidence rules apply at a Summary Court-Martial proceeding?

The Military Rules of Evidence (Mil. R. Evid.), found in Part III of the MCM, apply at a SCM proceeding. (paragraph 2-2)

What does a motion to dismiss address?

A motion to dismiss is a request to terminate further proceedings as to one or more charges and specifications on grounds capable of resolution without trial of the general issue of guilt. (paragraph 2-3)
