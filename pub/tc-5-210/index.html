TC 5-210 — Military Float Bridging Equipment

TC 5-210 governs the use of military floating equipment in support of river crossing operations.

TC 5-210 covers tactical employment, planning, and the design and construction of floating rafts and bridges. It also addresses anchorages, floating protective devices, fixed bridge spans, assembly rates, bridge classification, and equipment requirements.

Applies to: It is intended for use by engineer officers and noncommissioned officers at all levels.

Topics covered

  • River crossing operations
  • Ribbon bridge assembly
  • Floating rafts and bridges
  • Bridge anchorages
  • Balk depressors
  • Fixed span bridges
  • Floating bridge protective devices
  • External air transport procedures

Questions and answers

How are boats tied off to ribbon rafts?

The manner in which boats are tied off to ribbon rafts depends upon the method of rafting that is selected. (paragraph 8)

How are saddle adapters secured to the beam?

Each adapter is held in place by the sliding retainer lugs on the beam. (paragraph 8)

How many outboard motors normally propel an LTR?

Normally, four outboard motors will be used to propel an LTR although as few as two can be used in currents up to 5 FPS. (paragraph 7)

What must be done to the sling legs during helicopter hookup?

Tape the sling legs to prevent fouling during hookup. (paragraph 4)
