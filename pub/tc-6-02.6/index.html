TC 6-02.6 — Grounding Techniques For Tactical Equipment And Systems

TC 6-02.6 governs earth grounding methods and procedures for tactical systems, equipment, and shelters.

It covers earth, equipment, and chassis grounds; grounding rods, plates, wires, connections, and installation methods. It also addresses soil conditions, terrain, climate, soil resistance, and measures for improving grounding.

Applies to: TC 6-02.6 applies to the Active Army, Army National Guard, Army National Guard of the United States, and United States Army Reserves unless otherwise stated.

Questions and answers

What equipment uses a chassis ground?

A chassis ground is used in all electrical (electronic) equipment, vehicles, and generators. (paragraph 1-8)

How should clamp or screw connections be secured?

Clamp or screw connections tightly to prevent loosening over time. (paragraph 2-6)

How much surface contact must grounding plates have?

The plates must have at least three square feet of surface contact with ground (front and back of plate is 18 inches by 18 inches). (paragraph 2-21)

How far below the terrain should a ground rod be installed?

Ground rods should be Installed to full length so that top of ground rod is 3-5 inches below terrain. (paragraph 5-14)

When can chemicals be added to lower ground resistance?

If your ground resistance, as measured by a null-balance earth tester or equivalent device, is 25 ohms or more, you may add chemicals to lower the resistance. (paragraph 3-5)
