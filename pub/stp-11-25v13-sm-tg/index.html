It contains task summaries for still documentation, television production, and combat camera operations across skill levels 1, 2, and 3. The tasks specify conditions, standards, performance measures, and evaluation guidance for photographic, video, audio, digital imaging, and transmission work.

Applies to: This manual applies to both Active and Reserve Component soldiers.

Topics covered

  • Copy photography
  • Photographic chemistry
  • Photographic film processing
  • Television production
  • Combat camera operations
  • Digital image acquisition and editing
  • Video information transmission
  • Photographic processor chemistry

Questions and answers

What does the publication require for corrected photographic exposure?

Set corrected exposure (BE x BEF x FF = NE). (paragraph 9)

What safety precautions apply when mixing photographic chemicals?

When mixing, pour chemicals slowly into water to avoid splashing. Avoid breathing chemical dust or fumes. Always add acids to water. Also, follow all manufacturers safety regulations. (paragraph 9)

What must be confirmed after an electronic image transmission?

Confirmed transmission by calling JCCC or release authority. (paragraph 5)

Who has authority to task service COMCAM resources?

Only the National Command Authorities (NCA), Chairman of the Joint Chief of Staff (CJCS), Joint Staff, Unified Commanders, and specified Commanders (when not directly supporting a Unified Commander) have the authority to task service COMCAM resources. (paragraph 2)
