TC 3-22.37 — Javelin-close Combat Missile System, Medium

TC 3-22.37 governs the Javelin Close Combat Missile System, Medium, including its operation, training, and tactical employment.

  • Publication number: TC 3-22.37
  • Title: JAVELIN-CLOSE COMBAT MISSILE SYSTEM, MEDIUM
  • Date: 08/13/2013
  • Proponent: T2COM
  • Status: ACTIVE

View TC 3-22.37 on armypubs.army.mil

It covers the weapon system, command launch unit, missile operation, firing positions, carrying techniques, and Block 1 improvements. It also contains training programs, tactical considerations, target engagement techniques, safety procedures, and malfunction guidance.

Questions and answers

How long does the Javelin battery coolant unit operate?

The BCU is a single-use unit with 4 minutes of operating time; it is not rechargeable. (paragraph 1-31)

What does the Javelin guidance section do?

The guidance section (Figure 1-21) provides target tracking and flight control signals. (paragraph 1-34)

How does the Javelin achieve fire-and-forget capability?

The missile I2R system gives the missile its fire-and-forget capability. (paragraph 1-35)

What firing positions does the Javelin use?

Firing positions for the Javelin include: sitting (bent knees or legs crossed), the kneeling (using one knee or both knees), standing supported, and the prone supported. (paragraph 2-52)

How must a target be classified after detection?

Once a target is detected, the gunner must classify the target as either a wheeled or tracked vehicle (Figure 4-24). (paragraph 4-83)
