ATP 3-07.5 — Stability Techniques

It covers stability tasks and principles, conflict transformation, unity of effort, legitimacy, host-nation ownership, partner capacity, and the rule of law. It describes tasks for establishing security and civil control, restoring services, supporting governance, and developing economic and infrastructure systems.

Questions and answers

What stability principles apply to all primary stability tasks?

Five basic stability principles undergird stability efforts and apply to all primary stability tasks: Conflict transformation. Unity of effort and unity of purpose. Legitimacy and host-nation ownership. Building partner capacity. Rule of law. (paragraph 5-2)

How should Army units protect vulnerable groups during the initial response phase?

In the initial response phase, Army units protect vulnerable elements of the population. (paragraph 3-20)

What must Army units do to reopen schools during the initial response phase?

In the initial response phase, Army units repair and reopen schools as quickly as possible. (paragraph 4-97)

What must Army units do with penal administrative records?

In the initial response phase, Army units secure and preserve penal administrative records and reports. (paragraph 3-132)

What human rights violations must Army units prevent?

From the outset, Army units stay alert for and prevent human rights violations such as atrocities or torture. (paragraph 4-74)
