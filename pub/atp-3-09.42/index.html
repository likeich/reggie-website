ATP 3-09.42 — Fire Support For The Brigade Combat Team

Questions and answers

What information does reconnaissance provide?

Reconnaissance provides information concerning the disposition of an enemy force, the enemys intent, terrain conditions, and indications of chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear (CBRN) contamination. (paragraph 3-2)

What is the objective of field artillery task-organization?

The objective of field artillery task-organization is to ensure that each field artillery unit has an established command relationship or support relationship. (paragraph 6-213)

What does ATP 3-09.42 say about placing artillery in reserve?

Commanders should not place artillery in reserve. (paragraph 6-214)

What does the tactical command post do during airborne operations?

The tactical command post enhances mission command of maneuver and control of fire support. (paragraph 3-179)

How accurate are unmanned aircraft systems for attacking point targets?

Even under optimal conditions, unmanned aircraft systems will not be able to produce precise measured coordinates needed to attack point targets. (paragraph 3-180)
