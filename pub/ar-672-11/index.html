AR 672-11 — Brigadier General Jeremiah P. Holland Award

AR 672-11 governs policy and procedures for awarding the Brigadier General Jeremiah P. Holland Award to a military police unit each fiscal year.

AR 672-11 covers award concepts, nomination submission, selection, announcement, presentation, memorandum format, descriptive data, and assembly. It requires nomination packages to follow specified formats, enclosures, data methods, and submission deadlines.

Applies to: Commanders of eligible military police units may submit nominations for the award per this regulation.

Questions and answers

How many award nominations may each command submit per fiscal year?

Each ACOM, ASCC, DRU, the ARNG/ARNGUS, the USAR, and FOAs may submit one award nomination each fiscal year. (paragraph 2-3)

When must award nominations arrive at HQDA?

Nominations will be sent to HQDA (DAPMSM), Suite 1E596, 2800 Army Pentagon, Washington, DC 203102800, to arrive not later than 15 November. (paragraph 2-3)

What performance period is used to select the award winner?

Selection will be based on a units performance between the period of 1 October to 30 September, as measured by recorded accomplishments, achievements, and command programs that best exemplify the intent of the awards sponsor. (paragraph 2-2)

When will the award winner be announced?

The award winner will be announced not later than 15 December. (paragraph 2-4)

How must nominations be submitted?

Nominations will be submitted using a formal memorandum through the chain of command, with endorsements from the senior commander and provost marshal (if applicable) to HQDA (DAPMSM), Suite 1E596, 2800 Army Pentagon, Washington, DC 203102800. (paragraph 3-1)
