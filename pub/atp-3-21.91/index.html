ATP 3-21.91 — Stryker Brigade Combat Team Weapons Troop

ATP 3-21.91 governs the organization, capabilities, and employment of the Stryker Brigade Combat Team weapons troop.

  • Publication number: ATP 3-21.91
  • Title: STRYKER BRIGADE COMBAT TEAM WEAPONS TROOP
  • Date: 05/11/2017
  • Proponent: T2COM
  • Status: ACTIVE

It covers reconnaissance and security, offensive and defensive operations, stability, sustainment, and tactical enabling tasks. It describes troop duties, planning considerations, movement, fire control, logistics, maintenance, recovery, and casualty procedures.

Questions and answers

What does the signal support NCO supervise?

The signal support NCO supervises the operation, maintenance, and installation of organic digital, wire, and field FM communications. (paragraph 1-79)

What does planning a movement to contact emphasize?

Planning a movement to contact allows for flexibility and promotes subordinate initiative. (paragraph 1-80)

How does the commander protect the main body during a movement to contact?

The commander conducts information collection tasks to determine the enemys location and intent while conducting security operations to protect the main body. (paragraph 3-113)

What characteristics do successful defenses employ?

Successful defenses employ the characteristics of disruption, flexibility, maneuver, mass and concentration, operations in depth, preparation, and security (refer to FM 3-90-1 for more information). (paragraph 4-3)

What phases are included in the stability framework?

A stability framework based on conditions within the area of operations of initial response, transformation, and fostering stability, helps the unit determine the required training and task organization of forces prior to initial deployment, and serves as a guide to actions in an operation focused on stability tasks. (paragraph 5-2)
