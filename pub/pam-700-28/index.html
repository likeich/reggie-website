PAM 700-28 — Independent Logistics Assessments

It covers assessment progression, team selection, planning, timing, criteria, reporting, and process deliverables. Its assessment areas include supply support, maintenance, technical data, training, facilities, and computer resources.

Applies to: This pamphlet is intended for use by personnel involved in systems acquisition, materiel fielding or materiel transfer.

Questions and answers

What assessment supports the Milestone B decision?

An assessment of the supportability-related planning and activities in support of Milestone B (pre-acquisition). (paragraph 3)

When are additional independent logistics assessments conducted?

Additional ILAs may be conducted as required pre or post Milestone C based upon program requirements. (paragraph 3)

What does supply support include?

Supply support is all the management actions, procedures, and techniques used to determine requirements to acquire, catalog, receive, store, transfer, issue and dispose of secondary items. (paragraph 2)

What does maintenance planning and management cover?

Maintenance planning and management includes all logistics factors found in maintenance concept, design and analysis, maintenance planning and maintenance plans for the life of the system. (paragraph 2)
