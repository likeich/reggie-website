PAM 420-1-2 covers programming procedures, construction budgeting, funded projects, planning charrettes, and nonappropriated fund projects. It describes project justification, required data and reporting, form structure, consistency checks, and submission schedules.

Applies to: It is useful for all persons involved in Army construction program development and execution, from those who assist in providing data to those who make decisions using the results of the forms preparation.

Questions and answers

What happens if DD Form 1390 and DD Form 1391 are incomplete?

Lack of proper and complete DD Form 1390 and DD Form 1391 in support of programs and projects will result in deferral or elimination of those projects from the MILCON or NAF program. (paragraph 1-4)

When must full DD Forms 1391 be submitted?

Prior to 1 June of the GY, IMCOM regions must submit full DD Forms 1391 for each project in the fiscal years identified by the Army Guidance. (paragraph 1)
