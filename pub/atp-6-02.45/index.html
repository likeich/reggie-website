Questions and answers

What is the information environment in ATP 6-02.45?

The information environment is the aggregate of individuals, organizations, and systems that collect, process, disseminate, or act on information (JP 3-13). (paragraph 1-1)

What happens when spectrum congestion limits available bandwidth?

Insufficient resources caused by spectrum congestion may result in degraded capabilities in the information systems of U.S. forces and allies. (paragraph 1-5)

What limits high-capacity line-of-sight transmission systems?

Distance, terrain, and man-made obstructions are limiting factors when employing line-of-sight transmission systems at-the-halt. (paragraph 2-41)

How many command posts can an expeditionary signal company support simultaneously?

An expeditionary signal company can provide communications support to two medium command posts and ten small command posts simultaneously. (paragraph 3-34)

How do organizations without organic tactical communications assets request support?

Organizations without organic tactical communications assets request communications support through the RFF process when conducting theater operations. (paragraph 6-02)
