TC 21-24 — Tower And Aircraft Rappelling

TC 21-24 governs techniques, procedures, equipment, and safety requirements for tower and helicopter rappelling operations.

TC 21-24 covers rappelling personnel and duty positions, briefings, communications, aircraft procedures, rope management, equipment inspection, and knots. It requires equipment checks, rappeller preparation, safety measures, and specific procedures for UH-60 and CH-47 operations.

Applies to: The principal audience for TC 21-24 is all members involved with rappelling operations, whether resident or nonresident Soldiers.

Questions and answers

What must rappellers prepare before conducting a rappel?

Before conducting a rappel, every rappeller prepares their individual clothing and equipment. (paragraph 1-19)

When are rappelling materials and individual equipment inspected?

All rappelling material and individual rappelling equipment is inspected before conducting any rappelling operation. (paragraph 3-7)

When should rappelling ropes be inspected?

Ropes should be inspected before and after use. (paragraph 3-8)

What happens if a rope has certain deficiencies?

If any of the following deficiencies are identified, the rope is unserviceable. (paragraph 3-8)

Are night vision goggles worn during a night rappel descent?

Night vision goggles are not worn by rappellers during the descent. (paragraph 2-14)
