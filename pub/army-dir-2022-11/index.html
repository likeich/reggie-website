ARMY DIR 2022-11 — Designation Of Deputy For Medical Systems

ARMY DIR 2022-11 governs the designation of the Deputy for Medical Systems for the ASA (ALT) and related policy changes.

  • Publication number: ARMY DIR 2022-11
  • Title: DESIGNATION OF DEPUTY FOR MEDICAL SYSTEMS
  • Date: 09/16/2022
  • Proponent: ASA (AL&T)
  • Status: ACTIVE

View ARMY DIR 2022-11 on armypubs.army.mil

It supersedes paragraph 14m(3) of AR 701 and assigns the Deputy for Medical Systems functions formerly assigned to AMRMC. It also identifies the ASA (ALT) as policy proponent and sets rescission upon publication of the revised regulation.

Applies to: The provisions of this directive apply to the Regular Army, Army National Guard/Army National Guard of the United States, and U.S. Army Reserve.

Questions and answers

Who is designated as the Deputy for Medical Systems for the ASA (ALT)?

The Commanding General, United States Army Medical Research and Development Command (CG, USAMRDC) is hereby designated as the Deputy for Medical Systems for the ASA (ALT). (paragraph 4)

Which Army components does ARMY DIR 2022-11 apply to?

The provisions of this directive apply to the Regular Army, Army National Guard/Army National Guard of the United States, and U.S. Army Reserve. (paragraph 3)
