Questions and answers

What does the mace signal for a marching band?

Drum majors use the mace to signal commands to the marching band. (paragraph 2-2)

What tempo is used for drum major commands and mace signals?

Commands and mace signals are performed at a tempo between 112 -118 beats per minute, with 116 the preferred tempo for most occasions. (paragraph 2-4)

How do drum majors carry the mace?

Drum majors carry the mace in either the left hand at Left Carry or in the right hand at Right Carry. (paragraph 2-9)

What is the Trail mace carry used for?

The Trail is an alternate carry used primarily to move the mace into position to execute the Countermarch signal. (paragraph 2-15)

What is the Seven-Count Cutoff used for?

The Seven-Count Cutoff is used to stop playing a march and begin playing a cadence. (paragraph 2-45)
