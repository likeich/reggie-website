Reggie.Bot — AI search for US Army regulations

Reggie.Bot answers questions about US Army regulations, doctrine and publications, citing the governing regulation with paragraph and page references. It searches the full text of more than 1,500 publications from armypubs.army.mil, including Army Regulations (AR), Department of the Army Pamphlets (DA PAM), Army Techniques Publications (ATP), Field Manuals (FM), Training Circulars (TC) and Army Directives.

  • What are the rules on beards and grooming standards?
  • What is the Army body composition program?
  • How does nonjudicial punishment under Article 15 work?
  • What are the rules for emergency leave?
